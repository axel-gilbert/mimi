**Mimi 0.4.1** 🩹: fixes the GIF picker showing a big red cross.

## Fixed

- **GIF picker replaced by a red cross** after switching category or searching. Old previews were freed while the animation engine still knew about them, and the next frame crashed the grid. Previews are now released properly, each GIF is animated on its own, and the bar and the grid can no longer end up as a red cross even if something unexpected happens.

## Update

Right-click the heart icon → **Check for updates**, or wait: Mimi updates itself within a few hours.
