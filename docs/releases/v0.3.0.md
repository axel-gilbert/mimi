**Mimi 0.3** 💗: big hearts, sync, messages, pictures, GIFs, colours, do not disturb, 31 languages, and real encryption.

## What's new

- 💗 **Hold for a big heart**: hold the shortcut and a big heart grows on your partner's screen until you let go, then bursts. Holding no longer spams.
- 💞 **Sync**: press at the same time as your partner and two hearts meet in the middle.
- 💌 **Messages, pictures & GIFs**: **Ctrl + Alt + M** opens a bar in the middle of the screen. Write a message, add a picture (🖼, Ctrl+V or drag & drop, PNG with transparency supported) or search a GIF. It shows up in the middle of your partner's screen.
- 🎨 **Heart themes**: 8 colour presets or your own colour, glossy / flat / outline styles. Your partner sees your hearts in your colours.
- 🌙 **Do not disturb**: on/off, or for 10 min to 24 h (right-click the heart icon). Afterwards you see how many hearts you got, and the messages and pictures that arrived meanwhile.
- 📊 **Stats**: today, streak, last 7 days, totals.
- ✨ **New settings window** with tabs and live previews.
- 🌍 **31 languages**, picked automatically from Windows.
- 🔒 **Real security**: pairing now uses an ECDH key exchange; every message is encrypted and authenticated, with replay protection. The key is stored encrypted for your Windows account.
- 🤝 **Your existing pairing keeps working.** Pair again once both PCs have 0.3 for the strongest security.

## Install / update (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

- **Already on 0.2?** Nothing to do: Mimi updates itself within a few hours (or right-click the heart icon → *Check for updates*).
- **On 0.1 or new?** Download **`Mimi-0.3.0-win-x64.exe`** below, double-click it and click **Yes**. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*: **More info → Run anyway**.

Step-by-step guide in 31 languages: see the [README](https://github.com/axel-gilbert/mimi#readme).
